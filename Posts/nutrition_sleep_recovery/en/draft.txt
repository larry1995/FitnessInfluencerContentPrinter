SLEEP — YOUR #1 RECOVERY TOOL (AND IT'S FREE)

You can't out-supplement bad sleep. Here's what the research says about sleep and your gains:

1. GROWTH HORMONE PEAKS DURING DEEP SLEEP
During N3 (deep sleep), your body releases the biggest surge of growth hormone, testosterone, and IGF-1 — the three hormones most responsible for muscle repair and protein synthesis. Miss deep sleep, miss your gains window.

2. ONE BAD NIGHT TANKS YOUR TESTOSTERONE
A single night of sleep deprivation can reduce testosterone levels by nearly 25%. Chronic sleep restriction shifts your body into a catabolic state — breaking down muscle instead of building it.

3. STRENGTH AND POWER DROP MEASURABLY
A 2025 systematic review found that sleep deprivation significantly impairs anaerobic power, movement accuracy, and muscle strength. Athletes sleeping less than 7 hours showed measurable declines in squat and bench performance.

4. GLYCOGEN DOESN'T FULLY RELOAD
Sleep deprivation depletes muscle and liver glycogen and impairs glycogen resynthesis. This means you start your next session with less fuel in the tank — even if your nutrition is perfect.

5. INJURY RISK SKYROCKETS
Athletes sleeping fewer than 8 hours per night had a 1.7x higher injury rate compared to those sleeping 8+ hours. Sleep deprivation also impairs reaction time and motor coordination.

HOW TO OPTIMIZE YOUR SLEEP FOR GAINS:
- Target 7-9 hours per night (8+ is ideal for hard training)
- Keep a consistent sleep/wake schedule — even on weekends
- Cool, dark room (65-68F / 18-20C)
- No screens 30-60 min before bed
- Avoid caffeine after 2 PM
- If you train in the evening, allow 2-3 hours before bed
- Consider magnesium glycinate (200-400 mg) before bed

THE BOTTOM LINE:
Sleep is the most powerful recovery tool you have. No supplement, no ice bath, no compression boot comes close. Prioritize 8 hours like you prioritize your programming.

🏋️ THE BEST BEGINNER POWERLIFTING PROGRAM (12 WEEKS, FREE)

New to powerlifting? Stop program-hopping. Here's a 12-week linear progression that actually works — backed by research and used by hundreds of lifters at our gym.

WHY LINEAR PROGRESSION WORKS FOR BEGINNERS:
A 2017 meta-analysis of novice strength training found that simple linear models produced the SAME strength gains as complex periodization in the first 6-12 months. Translation: you don't need a complicated program yet. You need consistency and progressive overload.

THE STRUCTURE — 3 DAYS PER WEEK:

📅 DAY A — SQUAT FOCUS
A1. Back Squat — 3 x 5 (add 5 lbs each session)
A2. Bench Press — 3 x 5 (add 5 lbs each session)
A3. Barbell Row — 3 x 5 (add 5 lbs each session)
B1. Leg Press — 3 x 10
B2. Dumbbell Chest Flyes — 3 x 12

📅 DAY B — DEADLIFT FOCUS
A1. Deadlift — 1 x 5 (add 10 lbs each session)
A2. Overhead Press — 3 x 5 (add 5 lbs each session)
A3. Pull-ups or Lat Pulldown — 3 x 8
B1. Romanian Deadlift — 3 x 8
B2. Face Pulls — 3 x 15

📅 WEEKLY ROTATION:
Week 1: A / B / A
Week 2: B / A / B
Week 3: A / B / A (repeat)

STARTING WEIGHTS:
Start at 50% of your estimated 1RM for each lift. If you don't know your max, start with a weight you can do for 3 sets of 5 with 2-3 reps in reserve. The weight will get heavy fast.

WHEN TO DELOAD:
If you fail to complete all sets and reps for 2 consecutive sessions on a lift:
1. Reduce that lift's weight by 10%
2. Resume adding weight from there
3. If you stall 3 times, it's time for an intermediate program

PROGRESSION TIMELINE:
- Weeks 1-4: Weights feel easy. Trust the process.
- Weeks 5-8: Weights start to challenge you. Form stays priority #1.
- Weeks 9-12: Grinding reps. This is where strength is built.

A beginner male can expect to add 60-90 lbs to their squat in 12 weeks. Females typically add 30-50 lbs. These are real, achievable numbers from our coaching experience.

NUTRITION MINIMUM:
- Protein: 0.7-1.0g per lb bodyweight daily
- Calories: Eat at maintenance or a slight surplus (+200-300 kcal)
- Sleep: 7-9 hours per night

THE BOTTOM LINE:
Simple works. A 2023 systematic review found that untrained individuals gain significant strength from ANY progressive resistance program — the key variable is consistency, not complexity. Start here, follow the plan, and don't change programs for 12 weeks.

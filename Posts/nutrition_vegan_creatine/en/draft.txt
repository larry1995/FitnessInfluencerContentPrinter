VEGAN CREATINE — DOES IT ACTUALLY WORK?

Yes. And the science is overwhelming.

Creatine is the MOST studied sports supplement in history. Here's what a 2025 meta-analysis of 108 studies found:

THE RESEARCH:
Creatine users gained an average of 1.14 kg MORE lean body mass than those doing resistance training alone. They also lost 0.7 kg more body fat and reduced body fat percentage by 0.9%.

All creatine monohydrate is synthetically produced — meaning it's already vegan-friendly by default. You don't need a special "plant-based" label.

1. DOSE MATTERS
Higher doses (>5g/day) lead to more substantial improvements in lower-body strength. The standard recommendation: 5g daily, every day, no loading phase needed.

2. IT WORKS FOR BOTH UPPER AND LOWER BODY
Creatine significantly improves squat strength and bench press performance. It also enhances power output in vertical jump and anaerobic capacity.

3. SAFETY IS NOT A CONCERN
The most common side effect is temporary water retention in the first few days. Long-term studies show no kidney, liver, or cardiovascular risks in healthy individuals.

4. SKIP THE FANCY FORMS
Creatine HCl, buffered creatine, creatine ethyl ester — none outperform plain monohydrate in head-to-head studies. Monohydrate is cheaper and better researched.

5. THIRD-PARTY TESTING MATTERS
Look for NSF Certified for Sport or Informed Sport labels. These verify purity and confirm the product is free of banned substances.

THE BOTTOM LINE:
5g of creatine monohydrate daily. It's cheap, safe, effective, and backed by decades of research. No loading. No cycling. Just consistency.

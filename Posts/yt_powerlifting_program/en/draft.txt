🏋️ FREE 16-WEEK POWERLIFTING PROGRAM (Calgary Barbell)

Looking for a legit, science-based program that's completely free? Calgary Barbell just revised their 16-week competition prep program.

HERE'S THE STRUCTURE:

📅 PHASE 1 — HYPERTROPHY (Weeks 1-5)
• Higher volume, moderate intensity
• 4 days/week: 2 upper, 2 lower
• RPE 6-8 range
• Build your base of muscle and work capacity

📅 PHASE 2 — STRENGTH (Weeks 6-11)
• Volume drops, intensity climbs
• Competition lifts become priority
• RPE 7-9 range
• Accessories reduce to focus on weak points

📅 PHASE 3 — PEAKING (Weeks 12-15)
• Low volume, high intensity
• Heavy singles and doubles
• RPE 8-10
• Specificity is king — squat, bench, deadlift dominate

📅 WEEK 16 — MEET WEEK
• Openers only
• Taper and rest
• Trust the process

WHY IT WORKS:
✅ Progressive overload built in
✅ RPE-based so it auto-regulates to YOUR strength level
✅ Proven by hundreds of lifters in competition
✅ Balances volume and intensity across the training cycle
✅ Free. No paywall. No catch.

🏋️ TRAINING OVER 40? READ THIS.

Masters divisions in powerlifting start at age 40. But getting stronger doesn't stop there — you just have to train smarter.

Here's what changes (and what doesn't):

✅ WHAT STAYS THE SAME:
• Progressive overload still works
• Compound movements are still king
• Protein needs are actually HIGHER (aim for 1g/lb)
• Consistency beats intensity every time

⚠️ WHAT NEEDS TO CHANGE:

1️⃣ RECOVERY TAKES LONGER
You can still train hard — but you may need more rest days between heavy sessions. 3-4 days/week often beats 5-6.

2️⃣ WARMUPS MATTER MORE
Spend 10-15 minutes warming up properly. Your joints will thank you. Cold lifting over 40 is asking for trouble.

3️⃣ MANAGE VOLUME CAREFULLY
You can't absorb the same training volume as a 25-year-old. Focus on quality sets close to failure rather than piling on junk volume.

4️⃣ PRIORITIZE MOBILITY
Add dedicated mobility work 2-3x/week. Hips, thoracic spine, and shoulders are the usual problem areas.

5️⃣ DON'T SKIP DELOADS
Schedule a deload every 4-6 weeks. Your connective tissue recovers slower than your muscles.

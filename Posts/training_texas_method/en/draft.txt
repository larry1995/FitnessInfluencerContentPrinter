🏋️ THE TEXAS METHOD — THE BEST INTERMEDIATE POWERLIFTING PROGRAM (FREE)

Outgrown your beginner linear progression? You can't add weight every session anymore? Welcome to intermediate programming. The Texas Method is one of the most proven frameworks for lifters in this stage.

WHY THE TEXAS METHOD WORKS:
Instead of adding weight every session (which stops working after 4-8 months), the Texas Method uses a WEEKLY progression with three distinct training days that manipulate volume, recovery, and intensity across the week.

THE STRUCTURE — 3 DAYS PER WEEK:

📅 MONDAY — VOLUME DAY
The hardest day. High volume drives adaptation.

Squat: 5 x 5 @ 90% of Friday's 5RM
Bench Press (or OHP alternating weeks): 5 x 5 @ 90% of Friday's 5RM
Deadlift: 1 x 5 @ 90% of current 5RM (deadlift doesn't need 5x5)
Barbell Row: 3 x 8
Accessory: Dips or Close-Grip Bench 3 x 8-10

📅 WEDNESDAY — RECOVERY DAY
Light and easy. Promotes recovery without adding fatigue.

Squat: 2 x 5 @ 80% of Monday's weight
OHP (or Bench, alternating): 3 x 5 @ light/moderate
Pull-ups: 3 x bodyweight max
Back Extensions or GHR: 3 x 10
Ab work: 3 x 15

📅 FRIDAY — INTENSITY DAY
The PR day. Test yourself with a new personal record.

Squat: 1 x 5 (new 5RM — add 5 lbs from last Friday)
Bench Press (or OHP): 1 x 5 (new 5RM — add 5 lbs)
Deadlift: 1 x 5 (new 5RM — add 5-10 lbs)
Power Cleans: 5 x 3 (optional — replace with weighted pull-ups if preferred)

WEEKLY PROGRESSION:
- Add 5 lbs to squat and bench Friday PRs each week
- Add 5-10 lbs to deadlift Friday PRs each week
- Monday volume adjusts automatically (90% of new Friday PR)
- Expect 2-4 months of consistent weekly PRs before stalling

MODERN MODIFICATIONS (RPE-BASED):
The original Texas Method uses fixed percentages. Modern coaches add RPE for autoregulation:
- Volume Day: RPE 7-8 on set 5 of 5
- Recovery Day: RPE 5-6
- Intensity Day: RPE 9-9.5 on the single top set

This prevents grinding yourself into the ground on bad days while still pushing hard on good days.

WHEN TO STALL AND WHAT TO DO:
If you miss your Friday PR for 2 consecutive weeks:
1. Reset Friday weights by 5-10%
2. Build back up with smaller jumps (2.5 lbs)
3. If you stall again, switch Friday to triples (1 x 3) instead of fives
4. After 4-6 months, consider transitioning to a block periodization program

NUTRITION FOR INTERMEDIATE GAINS:
- Protein: 0.8-1.0g per lb bodyweight
- Calories: Slight surplus (+200-500 kcal) for best strength gains
- Sleep: 8+ hours — intermediate programming is more demanding than beginner
- Creatine: 5g daily

THE BOTTOM LINE:
The Texas Method has produced more intermediate PRs than almost any other program. It's simple, it's free, and it works. The secret isn't the program — it's executing it consistently for months, eating enough, and sleeping enough.

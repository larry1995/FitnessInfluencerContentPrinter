OMEGA-3s — THE RECOVERY SUPPLEMENT YOU'RE PROBABLY MISSING

Creatine and protein get all the attention. But omega-3 fatty acids might be the most underrated supplement for lifters. Here's the science:

1. LESS MUSCLE SORENESS
A 2024 systematic review found that omega-3 supplementation significantly reduced delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS) after high-intensity eccentric exercise. Less soreness = better training quality the next session.

2. LOWER MUSCLE DAMAGE MARKERS
In 3 out of 4 controlled studies, creatine kinase (CK) and lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) — both markers of muscle damage — were significantly lower in the omega-3 group compared to placebo.

3. INCREASED MUSCLE MASS
Subjects receiving omega-3s showed increases in total skeletal muscle mass AND appendicular (limb) muscle mass compared to placebo, with measurable gains in grip strength. This effect was most pronounced in older adults but applies across ages.

4. FASTER STRENGTH RECOVERY
A 2026 study in Nature Scientific Reports found that omega-3 supplementation increased EPA- and DHA-derived oxylipins (anti-inflammatory lipid mediators) and attenuated the loss of maximum voluntary contraction after exercise-induced damage.

5. ANTI-INFLAMMATORY WITHOUT THE DOWNSIDES
Unlike NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen), which can impair muscle protein synthesis and gut health, omega-3s reduce inflammation through natural pathways without blunting the adaptive response to training.

HOW TO SUPPLEMENT:
- DOSE: 2-4g combined EPA + DHA daily (aim for high-EPA products)
- TIMING: Take with a meal containing fat for better absorption
- FORM: Fish oil, krill oil, or algae oil (vegan option) all work
- FOOD SOURCES: Salmon (2g per 6oz), sardines, mackerel, herring
- DURATION: Benefits emerge after 4-8 weeks of consistent use

WHAT TO LOOK FOR ON THE LABEL:
- Combined EPA + DHA per serving (not just "fish oil")
- Third-party tested (IFOS, NSF, or USP certified)
- Molecular distillation for purity
- At least 500mg EPA + 500mg DHA per serving

THE BOTTOM LINE:
Omega-3s won't replace good training or nutrition. But if you're already doing those right, 2-4g of EPA+DHA daily is one of the best recovery investments you can make.

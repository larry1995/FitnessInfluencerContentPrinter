🏋️ 5 MISTAKES KILLING YOUR BENCH PRESS

Chad Wesley Smith and Marisa Inda (Juggernaut Training Systems) break down the most common bench press errors — and how to fix them.

1️⃣ WRONG GRIP WIDTH
Too wide = shoulder stress. Too narrow = weak lockout.
Fix: Ring finger on the ring is a solid starting point. Adjust from there based on your build.

2️⃣ NO LEG DRIVE
Your bench starts from the ground up. Flat feet with zero tension = wasted pounds.
Fix: Drive your feet into the floor. Think "push yourself away from the bar."

3️⃣ FLARED ELBOWS
Elbows at 90° puts your shoulders in a terrible position.
Fix: Tuck to ~45° on the way down. Let them flare naturally as you press up.

4️⃣ LOSING YOUR ARCH
A proper arch isn't dangerous — it shortens range of motion and protects your shoulders.
Fix: Set your arch before you unrack. Squeeze your shoulder blades together and DOWN.

5️⃣ BAD BAR PATH
The bar shouldn't go straight up and down. It should follow a slight J-curve.
Fix: Touch below your nipple line, press back toward your face to lockout over your shoulders.

SAMPLE BENCH DAY WORKOUT:
A1. Bench Press — 4 x 5 @ RPE 7-8 (focus on grip, arch, bar path)
A2. Paused Bench Press — 3 x 3 @ 80% (2-sec pause, eliminate bounce)
B1. Dumbbell Incline Press — 3 x 8-10 (controlled eccentric)
B2. Cable Flyes — 3 x 12-15 (stretch at bottom)
C1. Tricep Pushdowns — 3 x 12-15
C2. Face Pulls — 3 x 15-20 (shoulder health)

💡 Small fixes = big PRs. Film your next bench session and check for these.

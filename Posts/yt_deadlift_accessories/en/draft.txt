⚙️ YOUR DEADLIFT ACCESSORIES ARE HOLDING YOU BACK

Accessories are supposed to build your deadlift. But if you're doing them wrong, they're just burning energy.

Here are the most common accessory mistakes — and how to fix them:

1️⃣ ROMANIAN DEADLIFTS (RDLs)
❌ Mistake: Bending at the knees too much — turning it into a squat
✅ Fix: Push your hips BACK. Keep a slight knee bend but feel the stretch in your hamstrings. This is a hip hinge, not a squat.

2️⃣ BARBELL ROWS
❌ Mistake: Standing too upright and using momentum
✅ Fix: Torso at 45° or lower. Pull to your belly button. If you have to heave it, it's too heavy.

3️⃣ DEFICIT DEADLIFTS
❌ Mistake: Standing on too high a platform and losing your back position
✅ Fix: 1-2 inch deficit MAX. If your back rounds off the floor, the deficit is too big.

4️⃣ PAUSED DEADLIFTS
❌ Mistake: Pausing at the wrong position
✅ Fix: Pause at your sticking point (usually just below the knee). Hold for 2-3 seconds. No bouncing.

5️⃣ BLOCK PULLS
❌ Mistake: Using them to ego lift
✅ Fix: Use blocks to overload the lockout with 5-10% more than your max. Focus on hip drive and glute squeeze.

SAMPLE DEADLIFT ACCESSORY DAY:
A1. Conventional Deadlift — 4 x 3 @ RPE 7-8 (main lift)
B1. Deficit Deadlift — 3 x 4 @ 70% (1-inch deficit)
B2. Paused Deadlift — 3 x 3 @ 75% (pause below knee, 2 sec)
C1. Romanian Deadlift — 3 x 8-10 (hip hinge, hamstring stretch)
C2. Barbell Row — 3 x 8 (45-degree torso, no momentum)
D1. Block Pull — 2 x 3 @ 105% (overload lockout, hip drive)
D2. Farmer's Walks — 3 x 40m (grip endurance)

💡 RULE OF THUMB: Accessories should address a weakness, not just add fatigue. Know WHY you're doing each one.

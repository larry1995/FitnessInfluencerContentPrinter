🏋️ 5 MISTAKES KILLING YOUR SQUAT

Chad Wesley Smith and Marisa Inda break down the squat errors they see most — from beginners to experienced lifters.

1️⃣ DIVING INTO THE HOLE
Dropping fast feels strong, but you lose tension at the bottom and get stapled.
Fix: Control the descent. Own every inch. Think "slow down, push apart."

2️⃣ KNEES CAVING IN
Valgus collapse kills power out of the hole and puts your knees at risk.
Fix: Push your knees out over your toes. Cue "spread the floor" with your feet.

3️⃣ CHEST FALLING FORWARD
If the bar drifts forward, you turn your squat into a good morning.
Fix: Brace hard. "Big chest, big breath." Keep your upper back tight throughout.

4️⃣ WRONG STANCE WIDTH
Everyone's squat stance is different based on anatomy. Copying someone else's stance won't work.
Fix: Experiment. Your feet should be where your hips feel the most open and you can hit depth comfortably.

5️⃣ NOT BRACING PROPERLY
Breathing into your chest instead of your belly = no core stability.
Fix: Big breath into your belly. Push out against your belt (or where a belt would be). Hold it through the entire rep.

SAMPLE SQUAT DAY WORKOUT:
A1. Back Squat — 4 x 5 @ RPE 7-8 (focus on bracing and knee tracking)
A2. Pause Squat — 3 x 3 @ 75% (3-sec pause in the hole)
B1. Bulgarian Split Squat — 3 x 8 each leg
B2. Leg Press — 3 x 10-12 (feet high and wide for glute emphasis)
C1. Romanian Deadlift — 3 x 8-10
C2. Leg Curl — 3 x 12-15
D1. Ab Wheel Rollouts — 3 x 10 (bracing practice)

🔑 THE PATTERN:
Most squat problems come from losing tension. Stay tight from walkout to rerack.

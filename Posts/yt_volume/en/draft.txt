🏋️ MORE VOLUME ≠ MORE GAINS

The "more is better" approach to training volume is one of the biggest mistakes lifters make.

Revive Stronger breaks down when to add volume — and when to pull back.

THE PROBLEM:
Most lifters think: "If 10 sets builds muscle, 20 sets must build twice as much."

Wrong. Here's what the research shows:

📊 THE DOSE-RESPONSE CURVE:
• 0-10 sets/week per muscle: Strong growth response
• 10-20 sets/week: Diminishing returns — still grows but slower
• 20+ sets/week: For most people, recovery outpaces adaptation. You're just accumulating fatigue.

⚠️ SIGNS YOU'RE DOING TOO MUCH:
• Performance going DOWN over weeks (not just one bad day)
• Chronic joint soreness that doesn't resolve with warmups
• Sleep quality declining
• Motivation tanking — dreading the gym
• Strength stalling despite "working harder"

✅ THE SMARTER APPROACH:

1️⃣ Start with the MINIMUM effective dose (~10 sets/muscle/week)
2️⃣ Track your performance — are lifts going up?
3️⃣ Only add volume when progress stalls
4️⃣ Add 1-2 sets per week, not 5
5️⃣ Deload when fatigue accumulates (every 4-6 weeks)

💡 The best lifters don't do the most volume. They do the most PRODUCTIVE volume.

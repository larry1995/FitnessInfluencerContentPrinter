🏋️ YOUR WARMUP IS KILLING YOUR WORKOUT

A bad warmup either leaves you cold or burns you out before you even touch your working weight.

Here's how to structure warmups that actually help you perform:

1️⃣ START GENERAL (5 min)
Light cardio to raise body temperature. Don't overthink it — bike, row, or walk.

2️⃣ DYNAMIC MOBILITY (5 min)
Target the joints you'll use. Squatting? Hip circles, ankle rocks, bodyweight squats.

3️⃣ RAMP-UP SETS (the key part)
Start with the empty bar and work up in smart jumps:
• Bar × 10
• 40% × 5
• 60% × 3
• 75% × 2
• 85% × 1
• Working weight

4️⃣ USE THE RPE CHECK
Rate each warmup set:
💩 = felt worse than expected (slow down, add a set)
👍 = felt as expected (keep going)
🔥 = felt better than expected (you're ready)

PRO TIP: Your warmup should prepare you, not fatigue you. If you're breathing hard before your first working set, you did too much.

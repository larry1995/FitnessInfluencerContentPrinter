🏋️ WHAT 8,000 CALORIES LOOKS LIKE FOR A WORLD RECORD DEADLIFTER

Hafthor Björnsson — 2018 World's Strongest Man and 501kg deadlift record holder — recently had Dr. Mike Israetel evaluate his daily nutrition.

Here's what fueling a 6'9", 340lb strongman actually looks like:

🍳 BREAKFAST
Starts with 1.5L water, then eggs, oats, and fruit

🥩 MEALS 2-4
Massive portions of protein (chicken, beef, fish) paired with rice, potatoes, and sweet potatoes

🫐 SNACKS
Mixed berries, Greek yogurt, and protein shakes between meals

🍝 DINNER
Another large protein source with complex carbs and vegetables

THE TAKEAWAY FOR YOU:
You don't need 8,000 calories. But the principles are the same at any level:
→ Prioritize protein at every meal
→ Time carbs around your training
→ Stay hydrated (he starts with 1.5L before eating anything)
→ Eat whole foods as your base

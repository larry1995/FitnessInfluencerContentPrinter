CAFFEINE — THE BEST LEGAL PRE-WORKOUT (HERE'S THE SCIENCE)

Your morning coffee isn't just waking you up. It's making you stronger. Here's what the latest meta-analyses show:

1. MORE POWER OUTPUT
A 2025 meta-analysis of 12 studies (230 participants) found caffeine significantly increased mean power output (SMD = 0.21) and mean velocity (SMD = 0.42) during resistance exercises. Translation: faster, more explosive reps.

2. MORE STRENGTH AND ENDURANCE
A 2024 umbrella review of 14 meta-analyses confirmed that caffeine significantly improves both maximal strength AND muscular endurance. The effect was larger for compound lifts like squat and bench press.

3. THE OPTIMAL DOSE
3-6 mg per kg of bodyweight, taken 30-60 minutes before training. For a 180 lb (82 kg) lifter, that's roughly 250-500 mg — about 2-3 cups of strong coffee or one pre-workout scoop.

4. HABITUATION MATTERS
The biggest strength gains were seen in people who consumed LESS than 3 mg/kg/day habitually. If you drink 4 cups daily, your ergogenic boost will be smaller. Consider cycling off caffeine before competition.

5. SEX DIFFERENCES EXIST
Males showed greater improvements in mean velocity compared to females in the 2025 meta-analysis. This doesn't mean caffeine doesn't work for women — it does — but the magnitude may differ.

PRACTICAL GUIDE FOR LIFTERS:
- Dose: 3-6 mg/kg bodyweight, 30-60 min pre-training
- Best forms: coffee, caffeine pills, or pre-workout (all work equally)
- Avoid after 2 PM if it disrupts your sleep
- Cycle off for 7-10 days before a meet for maximum effect
- Stay hydrated — caffeine is a mild diuretic

THE BOTTOM LINE:
Caffeine is the most evidence-backed performance enhancer you can legally use. Dose it right, time it right, and your squat will thank you.
